Ribbon Bible Help

Follow the actual controls to read, find, and save passages. Bundled Bible editions, search, personal records, and reading plans remain available without an internet connection.

Start here

  1. Tap Read in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the current book and chapter at the top, then choose a passage.
  3. Tap a verse once to open its verse tools.

Read and move to a passage

  1. Tap Read in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the current book and chapter in the center of the top bar to open Go to passage.
  3. Choose from Recent, Old Testament, or New Testament, or enter an address such as John 3:16.
  4. With Vertical scroll, move up and down. With Swipe pages, swipe left and right.

Path: Read → current book and chapter at the top

Copy, share, and save a verse

  1. On the reading screen, tap the verse you want once.
  2. Use Copy, Share, Bookmark, Note, or Highlighter in the verse tools that appear at the bottom.
  3. Write your note and close the editor to save it. For a highlight, choose a named color and check its selected state.
  4. Open Library in the bottom navigation to see each saved item with its Scripture text.

Path: Read → tap a verse → verse tools at the bottom

Find a word, phrase, or reference

  1. Tap Search in the bottom navigation.
  2. Enter a word, part of a sentence, or a reference such as Gen 1:1.
  3. Tap a result to open that exact verse in the reading screen.

Path: Search → enter a query → tap a result

Track progress with Reading plans

  1. Tap Plans in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the plan name at the top, then choose a plan and start date.
  3. Tap the text row for a reading to open its passage.
  4. After reading, tap its checkbox. If that was a mistake, tap Undo in the saved message.

Path: Plans → choose a plan → tap the text row or checkbox

Library, backup, and restore

  1. Tap Library in the bottom navigation.
  2. Open Bookmarks, Notes, or Highlights. Tap an item to return to its Scripture text.
  3. Tap Back up to open the system share sheet, then save the JSON file in a location you trust.
  4. Tap Restore, select a JSON file previously exported by Ribbon Bible, review the record counts, and confirm.

Important: Restore replaces current records with the records in the backup. Back up the current records first. The backup is unencrypted plain-text JSON, not automatic cloud sync.

Reading settings, languages, and editions

Make reading comfortable

  1. Tap Aa at the top right of the reading screen.
  2. Adjust Text size, line spacing, and text width, then choose Vertical scroll or Swipe pages.
  3. Choose Light, Comfort, or Dark. Enable Keep screen awake only when you need it.
  4. To change the overall preset, open the top-left Menu → Reading mode and choose Standard reading, First-time reading, Pastor · Study, or Large type · Simple. Tap Aa to fine-tune individual settings.

Choose Scripture and app languages

  1. Tap Menu at the top left of the reading screen.
  2. Tap Language and editions.
  3. Choose the Bible language and edition and the app display language separately. Only editions bundled in the current build are shown.

Path: Read → Aa at top right / Read → Menu at top left → Reading mode or Language and editions

Large text, accessibility, and offline use

  • Large and simple increases both Scripture text and reading controls. Your device text-size setting is respected as well.
  • Choose Eye comfort or Dark to suit the room where you read.
  • Controls include functional labels for VoiceOver and TalkBack.
  • Bundled Scripture, search, Library, and Reading plans work offline.

Troubleshooting

A note or record is not saving.

Check the saved message, then close and reopen the app. Confirm that the device has free storage. Before deleting the app, back up the existing records in Library.

The restore file will not open.

Use a JSON file exported by Ribbon Bible and confirm that it is smaller than 16 MiB. If a newer app version created it, update the app and try again. A failed restore leaves the existing records unchanged.

I want to continue on a new device.

Download the app again from the purchases or library of the same Apple or Google store account. Personal records are separate from the purchase, so restore the backup exported from the old device in Library.

I cannot see the language or edition I want.

Open Menu → Language and editions from the reading screen. Only editions included in the current build after rights and quality checks are shown.

Search returns no results.

Try a few distinctive words instead of a long sentence. Enter a reference in book, chapter, and verse order, such as John 3:16.

Contact support

If the problem continues, include your device model, iOS or Android version, Ribbon Bible version, the buttons you tapped before the problem, and a screenshot.
