Ribbon Bible Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026

Ribbon Bible works without accounts, advertising, or analytics. The operator does not collect personal information or Bible reading history and does not send it to an operator-controlled server.

1. Information stored on your device

Your reading position and recent passages, reading settings, plan progress, bookmarks, notes, and highlights are stored locally. They are used only to provide app features.

2. External transfer and third parties

The app has no operator-controlled server and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK. Content leaves the app only when you explicitly copy or share it, export a backup, or import a restore file. The operating system may then pass it to the clipboard, file picker, or an app you choose. Nothing is sent to the operator.

3. Permissions and network use

Reading the Bible does not require an internet connection. The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access. Opening the public privacy-policy link uses the external browser you choose. Keep Screen Awake applies only while you read and only after you enable it.

4. Deletion and device backup

Bookmarks, notes, and highlights can be deleted individually. You can export or import settings, reading position, plan progress, and personal records as JSON. Exported backups are unencrypted plain-text files and may contain notes, bookmarks, highlights, and recent reading history. Check the destination and store them somewhere you trust. Deleting the app removes its local data, although an operating-system or store-account device backup may retain a copy or restore it to a new device.

5. Children and payment

The app does not use age-based advertising or profiling. Apple App Store or Google Play processes the purchase; the operator never receives or stores payment-card details.

6. Changes and contact

If app features or data practices change, this policy and the in-app notice will be updated together. Contact kyc123452@gmail.com.
